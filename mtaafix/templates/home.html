How it works

  1. 1

    Report it

    Describe the problem, add a photo and drop a pin on the map. MtaaFix suggests a category and checks if a neighbour already reported it.

  2. 2

    The authority acts

    The authority sets the priority and sends a field officer, who records progress on site.

  3. 3

    You are kept informed

    You get an email at every step, and the full history is always on your report.
